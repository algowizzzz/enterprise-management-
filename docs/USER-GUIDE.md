# Consilium quick start

Consilium brings your organisation's governance, risk and policy work into
one place: every committee, every policy and every escalated issue, with its
owner, its history and its next step.

This page gets you started in ten minutes. The full onboarding guide, with a
picture of every screen, is in [`guides/README.md`](guides/README.md).

---

## Signing in

Open the platform's address in your browser and sign in with your work
account. You arrive on the **Home** page. If you open a link while signed out,
you're asked to sign in and then taken straight to it.

## The screen

- **Search** (top of every screen): type part of a name, or press **/** first.
- **Menus**: **Home**, **My work**, **Governance**, **Policies**,
  **Escalations**, **Insights** and, for administrators, **Admin**. You see
  only the menus your role uses.
- **My work**: everything waiting on you. The number shows how many items.
- **A− / A+** make text smaller or larger; the moon switches to the dark
  theme.
- **Help** (bottom-right): ask anything about the screen you're on.

Details: [Finding your way](guides/01-finding-your-way.md).

## Your first five minutes

1. Open **My work** and see what's waiting.
2. Search for a committee you sit on and open it. Click through its tabs.
3. Open **Governance → Forum map** to see how committees connect.
4. Click **Help** and ask "What can I do here?".

## Common tasks

- **Request a new committee:** **Governance → Request a new forum** → fill in →
  **Submit for evaluation**.
- **Record minutes:** open the meeting → **Record minutes** → **Save
  minutes**.
- **Put a motion to a vote:** the committee's **Decisions** tab → **Put a
  motion**.
- **Vote:** open the motion → choose your ballot → **Cast my ballot**.
- **Answer an attestation:** **My work** → **Answer** → **Record answer**.
- **Approve something:** **My work → Approvals** → **Open** → record your
  decision.
- **Change a policy:** **Policies → Request a policy or change**.
- **Fix a wrong detail on a policy:** **Correct metadata** on the policy.
- **Raise an escalation:** **Escalations → Raise an escalation**.
- **Pick up an unowned escalation:** **My work → Escalations waiting for an
  owner** → **Take ownership**.
- **Get figures for a pack:** **Insights → Management reporting** → **Print**,
  or **Export CSV** on any list.

## Things worth knowing

- **Nothing is deleted.** Requests are withdrawn, seats ended, committees
  disbanded, policies retired and versions added. You can always see how
  things stood on any date.
- **You see only what you should.** Confidential policies and sensitive
  escalations are visible only to the people cleared for them.
- **A greyed-out button says why.** The screen, or Help's "Why can't I…?",
  tells you what must happen first.
- **Everything is recorded** for audit, including every time a rule stopped
  something.
