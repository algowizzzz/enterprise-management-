# 8. Your first day, by role

Find your role below. Each page lists what you'll use, a first-day checklist,
and your most common tasks as short steps, with a link to the full
explanation. Many people hold more than one role: read each page that applies
to you.

## 8.1 Board and committee members

**You'll use:** **My work**, the **Governance** menu (your committees' pages,
decisions and the forum map), and **Insights**.

**First-day checklist**

- [ ] Open **My work** and deal with anything waiting (votes, attestations).
- [ ] Find each committee you sit on (search its name) and read its
      **Details** and **Membership** tabs.
- [ ] Open the **Decisions** tab to see recent motions and sittings.
- [ ] Open **Home → Forum map** to see where your committees sit.
- [ ] Open **Insights → Management reporting**.

**Common tasks**

1. **Vote on a motion.** Open the motion from your link or the committee's
   **Decisions** tab → choose your **Ballot** → **Cast my ballot**
   ([3.9](03-governance.md)).
2. **Answer an attestation.** **My work** → **Answer** → choose your answer →
   **Record answer** ([2.3](02-my-work.md)).
3. **See who sat on a committee on a past date.** The committee's
   **Membership** tab → change **Membership as at** ([3.3](03-governance.md)).
4. **Get the figures for a meeting pack.** **Insights → Management
   reporting** → **Print** ([6.1](06-insights.md)).

## 8.2 The Chief Risk Officer and senior executives

**You'll use:** **My work** (approvals), **Escalations**, **Insights**, and
the forums you chair or sponsor.

**First-day checklist**

- [ ] **My work → Approvals**: decide what's waiting.
- [ ] **Escalations → Escalation register**: read **Waiting on you**.
- [ ] **Escalations → Breached**: matters past their time limit.
- [ ] **Insights → Gaps and risk** and **Emerging risks**.
- [ ] Bookmark **Insights → Management reporting**.

**Common tasks**

1. **Decide an approval step** (a policy, a new forum, a disbandment). **My
   work → Approvals** → **Open** → choose your **Decision** and give a reason →
   **Record decision** ([4.4](04-policies.md), [3.5](03-governance.md)).
2. **Decide a risk acceptance.** Open the matter → **Decide a risk
   acceptance** → *Approved* or *Rejected* → save ([5.6](05-escalations.md)).
3. **Resolve an exception or authorise a bypass** on a formation request.
   The request's **Approval** tab ([3.5](03-governance.md)).
4. **Pick up an escalation from your queue.** **My work → Escalations waiting
   for an owner** → **Take ownership** ([2.5](02-my-work.md)).
5. **See where risk is rising.** **Insights → Emerging risks**
   ([6.5](06-insights.md)).

## 8.3 Policy owners

**You'll use:** **Policies** (your policies, their versions, reviews and
monitoring), **My work**, and **Request a policy or change**.

**First-day checklist**

- [ ] **Policies → Policy library** → filter **Owner** to yourself.
- [ ] Open each policy and read its **Lifecycle** tab: what's next, and what
      would stop it.
- [ ] **Policies → Reviews due in 90 days**: plan your reviews.
- [ ] **My work**: any reviews or attestations due.

**Common tasks**

1. **Update a draft.** The policy's **Versions** tab → choose the file or type
   the text → **What changed, and why** → **Add the version**
   ([4.6](04-policies.md)).
2. **Send it for review.** **Submit for Review** → **Confirm**, then **Raise
   approval steps** on the **Approval** tab ([4.3](04-policies.md),
   [4.4](04-policies.md)).
3. **Ask for a change to a published policy.** **Policies → Request a policy
   or change** ([4.9](04-policies.md)).
4. **Conclude a periodic review.** The **Reviews** tab → **Conclude the
   review** ([4.10](04-policies.md)).
5. **Fix a wrong detail.** **Correct metadata** ([4.8](04-policies.md)).

## 8.4 Committee secretaries

**You'll use:** **Governance** (your committees), meetings and minutes,
motions, membership, charters, and **Request a new forum**.

**First-day checklist**

- [ ] Open each committee you support and check its **Membership** tab.
- [ ] Check the **Escalations** tab: matters the committee must consider.
- [ ] Check the **Decisions** tab: open motions and upcoming sittings.
- [ ] Check the **Documents** tab: is the charter cleared?

**Common tasks**

1. **Record minutes.** Open the meeting → **Record minutes** (or **Correct
   minutes**) → type or attach → **Save minutes** ([3.8](03-governance.md)).
2. **Put a motion.** **Decisions** tab → **Put a motion** → fill in → **Put
   the motion**; after the vote, **Record the outcome and close the motion**
   ([3.9](03-governance.md)).
3. **Take a new charter version.** **Documents** tab → **Take a new version**
   → **Publish the version** ([3.10](03-governance.md)).
4. **Request a new committee.** **Governance → Request a new forum** → **Save
   draft** → **Submit for evaluation** ([3.4](03-governance.md)).

## 8.5 The risk governance office

**You'll use:** everything in **Governance**, **Formation requests**,
attestation campaigns, and **Insights**. If you are also an administrator,
**Admin** too.

**First-day checklist**

- [ ] **Governance → Formation requests**: what's waiting on the office.
- [ ] **Governance → Awaiting compliance review** and **Overdue reviews**.
- [ ] **Policies → Attestation campaigns**: this year's progress.
- [ ] **Insights → Gaps and risk**.

**Common tasks**

1. **Evaluate a formation request.** Open it → **Start evaluation** → record
   each finding → **Save findings** → **Raise approval steps**
   ([3.5](03-governance.md)).
2. **Return a request with questions.** **Return to originator** → write the
   questions → **Return the request** ([3.5](03-governance.md)).
3. **Record the charter challenge.** Documents tab or request → **Record the
   outcome** ([3.10](03-governance.md)).
4. **Open an attestation campaign.** **Open a forum campaign** → **Open and
   generate tasks** ([4.10](04-policies.md)).
5. **Disband a forum.** **Disband this forum** → raise the plan → execute once
   approved ([3.11](03-governance.md)).

## 8.6 The enterprise policy office

**You'll use:** **Policies** (approvals, publication, exceptions), attestation,
regulatory updates, and **Insights**.

**First-day checklist**

- [ ] **Policies → In review**: what's waiting for approval.
- [ ] **Policies → Overdue reviews.**
- [ ] **Policies → Request a policy or change**: open requests.
- [ ] **Policies → Regulatory updates**: recent changes.

**Common tasks**

1. **Approve a policy.** Once the chain is complete: **Record Approval** →
   **Confirm** ([4.3](04-policies.md)).
2. **Publish and tell the audience.** **Publish** → **Confirm**, then
   **Record a publication** → preview **Who would be notified?** → **Record
   the publication** ([4.7](04-policies.md)).
3. **Approve an exception.** **Approval** tab → **Approve this exception**
   ([4.5](04-policies.md)).
4. **Classify a request.** Open it → answer the questions → **Classify**; then
   **Create the document** ([4.9](04-policies.md)).

## 8.7 Second-line reviewers

**You'll use:** **My work → Reviews**, compliance reviews of forums, policy
reviews, and escalation challenge.

**First-day checklist**

- [ ] **My work → Reviews**: everything waiting on you.
- [ ] **Governance → Awaiting compliance review.**
- [ ] **Escalations → Awaiting review.**

**Common tasks**

1. **Record a compliance review.** Open the forum → **Record a compliance
   review** → choose the decision → comments → **Record the decision**
   ([3.6](03-governance.md)).
2. **Record an annual review.** The forum's **Annual review** → choose the
   signed task and the decision → **Record the annual review**
   ([3.7](03-governance.md)).
3. **Challenge an escalation.** Open the matter → **Record a review round** →
   outcome and reasoning → save ([5.7](05-escalations.md)).
4. **Return a policy to drafting.** **Return to Drafting** with your comments
   ([4.3](04-policies.md)).

## 8.8 Escalation owners

**You'll use:** **Escalations**, **My work** (queues and action plans).

**First-day checklist**

- [ ] **Escalations → Escalation register**: your matters.
- [ ] **My work → Escalations waiting for an owner.**
- [ ] **Escalations → Breached**: anything past its time limit.

**Common tasks**

1. **Raise an escalation.** **Escalations → Raise an escalation** → fill in →
   check the proposal → **Raise the escalation** ([5.2](05-escalations.md)).
2. **Take ownership.** **Take ownership** in **My work** or on the matter
   ([5.4](05-escalations.md)).
3. **Add an action plan.** **Add an action plan** → **Add the plan**
   ([5.5](05-escalations.md)).
4. **Propose a risk acceptance.** **Propose a risk acceptance**, then
   **Request risk-acceptance approval** ([5.6](05-escalations.md)).
5. **Close a matter.** **Record the closure** → **Close the matter**
   ([5.9](05-escalations.md)).

## 8.9 Internal auditors

**You'll use:** read access across the platform, every record's **History**
tab, **Export evidence pack**, and **Insights**.

**First-day checklist**

- [ ] Open a forum, a policy and an escalation, and read their **History**
      tabs.
- [ ] Try **Export evidence pack** on one of them.
- [ ] **Insights → Management reporting** and **Gaps and risk.**

**Common tasks**

1. **Export evidence.** Open the record → **Export evidence pack**. The file
   has the record, its history, approvals, refusals, notifications and
   attachments, each with a fingerprint ([3.3](03-governance.md)).
2. **See who changed what.** The **History** tab → **Record history**.
3. **See a committee as it stood.** **Membership as at** on a past date
   ([3.3](03-governance.md)).
4. **Download figures.** **Export CSV** on any list, or **Download** on an
   analysis ([6.3](06-insights.md)).

You can read everything and change nothing.

## 8.10 Records managers

**You'll use:** retention and legal holds, and your own policies (such as the
records management standard).

**First-day checklist**

- [ ] Ask your administrator which retention classes and legal holds exist.
- [ ] Open your policies in the **Policy library**.

**Common tasks**

1. **Place a legal hold.** In the configuration screens, **Legal Hold** → add
   the hold reference, who requested and approved it, the date and what it
   covers → save. Records covered by a hold can't be changed or disposed of.
2. **Release a hold.** Set **Released On** on the hold.
3. **Review retention classes.** **Retention Class**: how long each kind of
   record is kept, and what happens at the end.

Your administrator can show you these screens ([chapter 9](09-administration.md)).
