# 10. Questions and answers

The questions people ask most in their first weeks, in plain language. If
yours isn't here, click **Help** on the screen you're on and ask it
([chapter 7](07-help-and-ai.md)).

---

## Getting around

**How do I find something quickly?**
Click the search box at the top of every screen, or press **/** on your
keyboard, and start typing. Forums, policies and escalations that match
appear as you type ([chapter 1](01-finding-your-way.md)).

**Where is everything waiting for me?**
In **My work**. The number on the **My work** menu tells you how many items
are waiting ([chapter 2](02-my-work.md)).

**A menu I expected isn't there. Why?**
You see only the menus your role uses. For example, only administrators see
**Admin**, and a board member doesn't see **Escalations**. If you think you
need more, ask your administrator.

**Can I send a colleague a link to a filtered list?**
Yes. Filters are kept in the page address, so a copied link opens the same
list for them. They still see only what they're allowed to.

**The text is too small (or too big).**
Use **A+** and **A−** at the top of the screen. The **↺** button goes back to
the normal size. The platform remembers your choice on this computer.

**Can I use it on my phone?**
Yes. On a narrow screen the menus move into a slide-out panel behind the menu
button at the top left.

## Why can't I…?

**Why is a button greyed out?**
Something has to happen first. The screen usually says what, right under the
button (for example "Record Approval would be refused today" and why). If it
doesn't, click **Help** and ask "Why can't I …?".

**Why can't I see a record someone sent me?**
Either the link is wrong, or the record is restricted, for example a
**sensitive** escalation or a **confidential** policy. The platform
deliberately doesn't say which. Ask the person who sent it.

**Why can't I edit this forum?**
Either you're not its owner, secretary or part of the governance office, or
the forum is **locked** while it waits for a compliance review. The forum's
page tells you which.

**Why can't I approve this?**
Approvals go to the people named on the approval route. If the step isn't
yours, you won't see a decision form. If the steps must be taken in order, an
earlier step may still be waiting. The **Approval** tab shows every step and
who it's with.

**Why can't I close this escalation?**
Record the closure first: how it ended, a summary, and each closure
criterion with its evidence. Then **Close the matter** is offered
([chapter 5](05-escalations.md)).

**Why can't I answer this attestation any more?**
It passed its due date and expired overnight. Ask the governance office to
send you a new one.

**Where do I answer the questions on my formation request?**
Open it from **My work** (**Formation requests returned to you**), write your
answer and click **Send your answer**. You don't need any special role to
answer a request you raised ([chapter 3](03-governance.md)).

## How do I…?

**How do I request a new committee?**
**Governance → Request a new forum**. Fill in the four sections, save a draft
whenever you like, then click **Submit for evaluation**
([chapter 3](03-governance.md)).

**How do I record a committee's minutes?**
Open the meeting and click **Record minutes** (or **Correct minutes**). Type
them or attach a file, then save. Earlier versions are kept
([chapter 3](03-governance.md)).

**How do I put a motion to a vote?**
On the forum's **Decisions** tab, click **Put a motion**. Members vote on the
motion's page; the secretary then records the outcome
([chapter 3](03-governance.md)).

**How do I change a policy?**
**Policies → Request a policy or change** for a formal change request. If the
policy is still in Draft, its owner can simply upload a new version
([chapter 4](04-policies.md)).

**How do I fix a wrong detail on a published policy?**
Use **Correct metadata** on the policy's page. It changes the detail, keeps the
reason, and doesn't send the policy back through approval
([chapter 4](04-policies.md)).

**How do I raise an escalation?**
**Escalations → Raise an escalation**. The platform proposes a severity and
where it goes as you fill in the form ([chapter 5](05-escalations.md)).

**How do I pick up an escalation nobody owns?**
**My work → Escalations waiting for an owner**, then **Take ownership**
([chapter 2](02-my-work.md)).

**How do I get figures for a committee pack?**
**Insights → Management reporting**. Every figure opens the list behind it,
tables have **Download** or **Export CSV** buttons, and **Print** gives a
clean paper copy ([chapter 6](06-insights.md)).

**How do I give someone my approvals while I'm away?**
Ask your administrator to set up a delegation for the dates you're away.
Anything your delegate does is marked "under delegation".

## Records and history

**Can I delete something I created by mistake?**
No. The platform is an audit record, so nothing is deleted. Instead you
withdraw a request, end a seat, disband a forum, retire a policy or record a
new version. Everything stays readable, as it stood on any date.

**How do I see who changed something?**
Open the record's **History** tab. **Record history** lists every change,
comment, attachment and notification, newest first.

**What does an auditor get?**
Auditors can click **Export evidence pack** on a forum, policy or escalation.
It downloads the record, its history, approvals and attachments as one file,
with a fingerprint for every item so nobody can alter it unnoticed.

## Notifications

**Why didn't I get an email?**
Your administrator decides how notifications are sent. If email isn't set
up, notifications are still recorded in the platform, and everything waiting
on you is always in **My work**.

**Can I change the wording of a notification?**
Your administrator can ([chapter 9](09-administration.md)).

## The Help assistant and AI

**Is the AI reading my confidential documents?**
No. It never sees confidential, restricted or sensitive records. What it may
see is set by your administrator, and every request to it is recorded
([chapter 7](07-help-and-ai.md)).

**The Help answer seems wrong.**
Check the sources listed under the answer; they link to the guide section it
used. Tell your governance office, who can improve the guidance.
