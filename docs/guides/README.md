# Consilium onboarding guide

Welcome to Consilium, your platform for **governance, risk and policy**. This
guide takes you through it screen by screen: every menu, every tab and what
each button does, with a picture of each step. Numbered markers ①②③ on the
pictures match the explanations beneath them.

Start with chapters 0 and 1, then go to **your first day** in chapter 8. It
tells you which chapters matter for your role.

## How to read this guide

- **Bold words** are exactly what you'll see on screen: a button, a tab, a menu
  or a field. For example: click **Save draft**.
- **Menu → item** means open the menu in the header, then choose the item. For
  example: **Governance → Forum inventory**.
- **①②③** match the orange numbered markers on the picture above them.
- You see only what your role allows. If a button in this guide isn't on your
  screen, your role doesn't use it. [Chapter 8](08-your-first-day.md) lists
  what each role does.
- The pictures come from the demonstration site. Your organisation's name,
  logo and colours will replace the demonstration branding, and the people in
  the pictures (such as *Chief Risk Officer (Demo)*) are demonstration users.

## Need help right now?

Click **Help** in the bottom-right corner of any screen and ask in your own
words, for example "How do I record minutes?". The answer shows which part of
this guide it came from ([chapter 7](07-help-and-ai.md)).
