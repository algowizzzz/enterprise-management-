Before you start

What this form does, and what happens after you send it.

Raising a matter records it open. The escalation matrix sets its severity and the forums it goes to, unless you set the severity yourself — which is recorded as your judgement. The template for the escalation type decides what else is required at that severity. The forums' chairs, secretaries and attesting members are told if the matter breaches its time limit or affects a material entity.
