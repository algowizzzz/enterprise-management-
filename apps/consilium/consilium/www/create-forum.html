Before you start

What this form does, and what happens after you send it.

This form does not create a forum. It raises a formation request. The forum comes into existence only when the request is evaluated against the governance criteria and approved; it then starts in draft and must pass a compliance review before it counts as compliant. Expect questions back; that exchange stays on the record.
  1. 1 You draft it
  2. 2 Submitted
  3. 3 Evaluated
  4. 4 Approved
  5. 5 Forum created, then reviewed

What you must provide

  • The kind of request, and for a change or retirement, the forum it concerns
  • The forum's proposed name and type
  • Why it is needed, what it will cover and what it will be responsible for
  • Who delegates the authority, and who sponsors it
  • The accountable operating group
  • The date you need it in place by

What helps, but is not required

  • A proposed reference, if your area uses its own numbering
  • The parent forum it will report into
  • The meeting cadence and the intended first meeting date
  • The line of business beneath the operating group

Check the inventory first. A request that duplicates a forum already covering the ground is the most common reason one is sent back.
